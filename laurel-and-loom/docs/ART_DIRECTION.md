# Laurel & Loom — Art Direction

Neoclassicism was a revival: eighteenth-century painters and potters looking back at Greece
and Rome and choosing restraint over ornament. The game borrows that temper. Calm marble
surfaces, strong horizontals, symmetry, and one accent colour doing the work at a time.

## Reference points

- **Jacques-Louis David** — *Oath of the Horatii*: a shallow stage, figures in a frieze, a
  single decisive gesture. Our battle banners and dialogue scenes are staged like this.
- **Wedgwood jasperware** — white relief figures on matte blue. Our player units.
- **Attic black-figure pottery** — black silhouettes on terracotta. Our enemy units.
- **Roman inscriptional capitals** (Trajan's Column) — our display type.
- **The Greek key (meander)** — our frame ornament.

Acted units desaturate to **grey marble** — as if turned to statues until the next phase.

## Typography

- **Cinzel** — Roman capitals for titles, banners, names and numbers on beads.
- **Cormorant Garamond** — body text, dialogue, descriptions.
- Turn counts use Roman numerals: *TURN IV*.

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License; the licence ships in `game/assets/fonts/`.

## Units: cameos

Each unit is an oval medallion seen from above the board:
- A rim (gold for lords and commanders, bronze otherwise).
- A field — jasper blue (player) or terracotta (enemy).
- A profile bust in relief — white on blue, black on terracotta — facing the unit's side of
  the field, with a class mark: a crest for soldiers, a laurel for the Augur, a veil for the
  Vestal, a bow or axe at the shoulder.
- A thin HP arc along the lower rim.

## Tiles

Seen straight down, drawn with flat fills and a few confident lines:
- **Plaza** — marble slabs with faint veins, alternating very slightly in tone.
- **Meadow** — laurel green with small sprigs.
- **Olive grove** — clusters of grey-green canopies with a cypress-shadow underneath.
- **Colonnade** — a fluted column drum seen from above, with its shadow.
- **Pool** — lapis with a fine ripple line and a marble kerb where it meets land.
- **Wall** — ashlar courses.
- **Altar** — a raised marble dais with a gold ring.
- **Gate** — two pylons with a laurel lintel.

## UI

- Panels are marble with a thin gold rule and a meander band on the long edge.
- The **thread bar** is a gold thread running across the top; beads hang from it like
  loom weights. Exact beads show their number, omen beads show a tint, unknown beads are
  plain.
- The **phase banner** is a frieze strip across the centre: *TURN II · PLAYER PHASE*.
- The **combat forecast** is laid out like a pediment: two columns, a centre line of strikes
  with the bead each will draw.

## Motion

Short and weighty, never bouncy. Units slide tile to tile. Attacks are a lunge and return.
A critical is a still frame and a flash of gold. A fallen unit cracks and fades to marble dust.

## Sound

A plucked lyre (Karplus–Strong synthesis) for interface sounds, in the Dorian mode; a low
drum for the enemy phase. All generated by script — no sample libraries.
